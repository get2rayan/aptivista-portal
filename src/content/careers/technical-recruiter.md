## About the role

You'll own the talent pipeline for a portfolio of client engagements — sourcing, screening, and shortlisting engineers and seeing them through to onboarding. We want recruiters who understand technology well enough to screen for it.

## Responsibilities

- Run the full-cycle pipeline: requirement, source, screen, shortlist, interview, onboard.
- Build and maintain a warm bench of engineering talent.
- Partner with clients to translate role needs into precise screens.
- Keep time-to-shortlist low without lowering the bar.

## Requirements

- 3+ years of technical or engineering recruiting.
- Comfortable screening for technical fundamentals.
- Strong organizational and communication skills.
