## About the role

You'll embed with a client engagement to own architecture and delivery decisions — from tech-stack choices to team operating model. This is a senior, hands-on role for someone who has carried critical builds to production.

## Responsibilities

- Assess current-state architecture and recommend a pragmatic path forward.
- Make and defend tech-stack and delivery decisions.
- Coach the client's team; raise the engineering bar.
- De-risk the few decisions that determine the outcome.

## Requirements

- 8+ years of software engineering, with significant architecture experience.
- Track record shipping production systems at scale.
- Excellent written and verbal communication with executives.
