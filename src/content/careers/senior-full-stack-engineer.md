## About the role

You'll own features across the stack for client engagements — from discovery to delivery — working closely with designers and the client's engineering team. We're looking for engineers who care about the whole product, not just their slice of the stack.

## Responsibilities

- Design and build features across frontend and backend using TypeScript, Astro, React, and Node.
- Write meaningful tests and own quality, performance, and accessibility.
- Partner directly with clients; communicate trade-offs clearly and honestly.
- Mentor junior engineers and raise the bar on craft.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of full-stack engineering experience.
- Strong TypeScript and Node.
- Experience shipping production web applications end to end.
- Comfortable working directly with stakeholders.

## Nice to have

- Experience with Astro or other content-driven frameworks.
- Background in regulated industries.
- Open-source contributions.
