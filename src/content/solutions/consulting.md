## What it is

We embed alongside your team to advise on architecture, delivery, and engineering org design — or lead a specific initiative end to end when you need senior hands on the wheel.

## Who it's for

Product and engineering leaders facing a critical build, a platform migration, a turnaround, or a scaling moment where the wrong call is expensive. You want a senior perspective, not a deck.

## How we engage

We start with an honest assessment, agree on a small set of decisions that matter most, and either advise or embed to execute. No filler — just the few moves that change the outcome.
