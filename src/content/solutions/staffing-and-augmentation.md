## What it is

We source, screen, and place engineering talent for your open positions — whether you need to backfill a departing role, augment a team for a sprint, or scale a new initiative from zero.

## Who it's for

Hiring managers and talent leaders who need qualified engineers in seat quickly, without spending weeks on sourcing and screening themselves. We take the pipeline off your desk so your team can keep shipping.

## How we engage

Every engagement follows the same disciplined pipeline: **Requirement → Source → Screen → Shortlist → Interview → Onboard → Support**. You meet only candidates who have already cleared a structured technical screen, and every placement comes with a documented onboarding checklist so new hires ramp fast.

## Outcomes you can expect

- Time-to-shortlist measured in days, with 2–3 vetted candidates per role.
- Lower interview burden on your team — only qualified people reach your calendar.
- Flexible engagement models: contract, contract-to-hire, and direct-hire.
- A single point of contact owning the loop end to end.
